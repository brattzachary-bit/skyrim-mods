Modified by zbratt, November 2026. Summary of changes (marked in the source with the tags shown):
  [Seamless]  the last frame of the old cell is held and dissolved into the new cell
  [EaseIn]    the held picture is taken with the HUD hidden and pushes forward very slowly
  [FGPause]   FidelityFX frame generation is paused while a transition is on screen
  [TravelFx]  transitions instead of the loading screen for loads that are not doors
  [Styles]    transition styles (eyes, fade, light, drowsy, ink, soul mist, wipe, dream, dissolve), MCM page, previews
  [Rules]     transitions chosen per door type, place and situation; user rule files
  [Safe]      start-up self-check; frames are never withheld unless a working overlay owns the screen;
              covered loads may take as long as they need
  [DLSSG]     experimental pause of NVIDIA DLSS frame generation (Streamline)
  [Compat]    Loading Menu open/close events are announced to other mods although the menu stays closed
  [Hotkey]    optional key tap at the start and end of every transition, for other mods' hotkeys
  include/VoidOverlayShader.h is new (shaders compiled at runtime).
